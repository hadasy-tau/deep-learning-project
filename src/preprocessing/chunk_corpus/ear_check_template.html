35 chunks from the 20 pilot sessions, two per session where there were two — one from near the start and one from near the end, because a constant timing offset shows in either but drift only shows by comparing them.

Play each clip and decide one thing: do the words you hear match the text beside it? You are not judging transcription quality or whether the protocol is verbatim — only whether the audio was cut from the right place. A chunk of fluent Hebrew that has nothing to do with its text means the m4a and the alignment disagree about where zero is, and that is invisible to every automated check in the pipeline.

Clips were picked at quality ≥ 0.5 so a mismatch means a timing fault rather than an alignment the score already flags. Verdicts save as you go and are shared with anyone who opens this page.
